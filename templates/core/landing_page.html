About C-HUK

The Centre for High Performance Computing Upskilling and Knowledge Sharing (C-HUK), established by C-DAC Bengaluru under the Human Resource Development initiative of the National Supercomputing Mission (NSM) and the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India, promotes inclusive skill development in High Performance Computing (HPC) and emerging technologies.

C-HUK offers expert-led training in HPC, Artificial Intelligence, Embedded Systems, Cyber Security, Faculty Development Programmes, and blended learning to empower learners from diverse backgrounds with industry-relevant technical skills.

About C-DAC

The Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC) is the premier Research and Development organization of the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India.

C-DAC is dedicated to advanced computing, High Performance Computing, Artificial Intelligence, Cyber Security, Embedded Systems, Software Technologies, Digital Education, Healthcare, and e-Governance, contributing significantly to India's technological growth and digital transformation.

About NSM

The National Supercomputing Mission (NSM) is a flagship initiative of the Government of India jointly implemented by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) and the Department of Science and Technology (DST).

The mission aims to strengthen India's supercomputing ecosystem by establishing world-class computing infrastructure, developing indigenous technologies, promoting research, and building skilled human resources in High Performance Computing and emerging technologies.
